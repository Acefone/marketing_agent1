# Journey Mapper Skill

## Identity and Scope

Skill responsible for mapping each collateral request to a stage in the **AceX Marketing Funnel Framework**. Produces a locked Journey Card that drives the drafter's content angle and the gamma-render layer's CTA pattern.

Invoked at **Step 6 of CLAUDE.md**, after brand voice extraction and brief collection.

This file is the canonical record of the AceX Marketing Funnel Framework.

---

## The 5 Stages

### Stage 01 — Awareness
**Problem Awareness & Category Education**

Ops managers don't know a no-code voice AI platform exists. Reach them before they search for solutions.

- **Goal:** Impressions, traffic, brand recall among ICP personas
- **Topic buckets:**
  - AI in contact centers — State of AI adoption, what's changing in 2026
  - Cost of manual ops — ₹150/call reality, agent attrition, seasonal spikes
  - Voice AI 101 — How voice bots work, STT/LLM/TTS explained simply
  - Industry pain points — Collections NPAs, COD verification, BPO RFPs
- **Signature angles:**
  - "Why your ops team needs AI, not your dev team"
  - "The hidden cost of COD confirmation calls"
  - "What BFSI collections looks like at ₹200/call"
  - "How BPOs are winning AI RFPs in 2026"
  - "Voice AI without Twilio in India — is it possible?"
- **Channels:** LinkedIn organic · SEO blog · LinkedIn ads · Industry newsletters · Quora/Reddit · Google Display · YouTube Shorts

---

### Stage 02 — Consideration
**Solution Evaluation & Differentiation**

Prospect is comparing options — Vapi, Retell, build-in-house, hire agents. Position AceX as the obvious choice for non-technical ops managers.

- **Goal:** MQLs, demo requests, content downloads
- **Topic buckets:**
  - Competitor comparisons — AceX vs Vapi, vs NICE, vs build in-house
  - No-code differentiation — Why developer platforms fail ops managers
  - India-specific advantages — DPDPA, TRAI compliance, data residency, latency
  - ROI frameworks — How to calculate voice bot ROI for your use case
- **Signature angles:**
  - "Vapi vs AceX: what ops managers don't get told"
  - "Why Genesys takes 18 months (and AceX takes hours)"
  - "Can Twilio-based voice bots be DPDPA compliant?"
  - "Voice bot ROI calculator: ₹150 → ₹50 per call"
  - "Build vs buy voice AI in 2026 — the real math"
- **Channels:** SEO comparison pages · LinkedIn thought leadership · Google Search ads · Retargeting · Email nurture · G2/Capterra · Webinars

---

### Stage 03 — Intent / Conversion
**Demo, Trial & POC Stage**

Prospect is close to deciding. Remove friction, address objections, prove capability fast. The 48-hour deploy claim needs to be felt, not just read.

- **Goal:** Demo bookings, POC starts, SQLs
- **Topic buckets:**
  - Use-case walkthroughs — Step-by-step: COD bot, collections bot, BPO bot
  - Objection handling — Security, compliance, latency, integration concerns
  - Demo assets — Video walkthroughs, live call recordings, sandbox access
  - Proof points — POC results, benchmark data, latency stats
- **Signature angles:**
  - "Build your first COD confirmation bot in 20 minutes"
  - "AceX security: what your CISO needs to know"
  - "How our AI evaluators let you test before going live"
  - "500ms latency explained: why it matters for collections"
  - "Sample call: AceX DPD collections agent in Hindi"
- **Channels:** Landing pages · Demo follow-up email · LinkedIn DMs (ABM) · YouTube product demos · Sales deck · WhatsApp outreach · Competitor retargeting

---

### Stage 04 — Onboarding & Activation
**First Value & Platform Adoption**

New customers need to reach "first agent live" fast. Every day of delay is churn risk. The 48-hour claim is your activation target.

- **Goal:** Time-to-first-agent, feature adoption, expansion signals
- **Topic buckets:**
  - Getting started guides — Step-by-step for each ICP use case
  - Feature education — Tool calling, AI evaluators, multi-language setup
  - Expansion triggers — When to add inbound, PCA, Contact Center Studio
  - Best practices — Prompt writing, success metric setup, monitoring
- **Signature angles:**
  - "Your first 48 hours with AceX: a checklist"
  - "How to write a system prompt that actually works"
  - "Setting up AI evaluators: a 15-minute guide"
  - "When to escalate to a human — designing handoff logic"
  - "Adding tool calling: SMS, CRM fetch, callbacks"
- **Channels:** In-product tooltips · Onboarding email sequence · Help docs · Loom walkthroughs · Customer success calls · Slack community

---

### Stage 05 — Retention & Advocacy
**Expansion, Proof & Referrals**

Happy customers expand to PCA, Contact Center Studio, Campaigns. They become case studies, G2 reviewers, and referrers into peer networks.

- **Goal:** NRR >100%, case studies, reviews, referrals
- **Topic buckets:**
  - Customer success stories — ROI proof by ICP: e-comm, BPO, BFSI
  - Platform expansion content — Voice Bot + CCS, Campaigns + Voice Bot combos
  - Community & events — Ops manager peer groups, webinars, AMAs
  - Review generation — G2, Capterra, LinkedIn testimonials
- **Signature angles:**
  - "How [BPO name] won 3 RFPs using AceX in 60 days"
  - "Collections at scale: Campaign + Voice Bot workflow"
  - "From voice bot to full contact center — one vendor"
  - "Measuring voice bot ROI with Post Call Analytics"
  - "AceX user stories: ops managers share what changed"
- **Channels:** Case study landing pages · LinkedIn customer spotlights · G2/Capterra campaigns · Referral program · QBR decks · Email expansion sequences · Community events

## Strategic Priority

Stages **02 and 03** are the current highest-leverage focus — 10+ active demos in pipeline mean mid-funnel conversion content is the priority: comparison pages, objection-handling assets, sample call recordings by ICP.

When a request could map to multiple stages, prefer **02 or 03** unless the brief clearly points elsewhere.

Stage **01** runs in parallel but must be **segmented by ICP from day one**. E-commerce, BPO, and BFSI ops managers respond to entirely different pain framing.

Stage **05** is time-sensitive despite being post-purchase. The first 2 POC customers are the most valuable marketing asset — a case study live before the next sales cycle changes how deals close.

## Doc-Type → Stage Defaults

| Doc Type | Default Stage | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Ebook — beginner guide / "101" / explainer | 01 — Awareness | Category education, no purchase intent yet |
| Ebook — buyer's guide / comparison | 02 — Consideration | Reader is actively evaluating |
| Ebook — playbook / implementation guide | 04 — Onboarding | Activation aid for new customers |
| Brochure — use-case bundle (3-page) | 02 — Consideration | Comparing solutions for a defined use case |
| Flyer — product flyer (2-page, proof-led) | 03 — Intent | Demo-ready prospect, proof-heavy |
| Flyer — sales one-pager (1-page, ICP-pinned) | 03 — Intent | Closer-stage, single ICP focus |
| Flyer — event handout | 01 — Awareness | First-touch awareness |
| Case study | 02 → 03 (MOFU → BOFU) | Proof for evaluation or final decision |
| Slide deck — first pitch | 02 — Consideration | Solution overview, evaluation support |
| Slide deck — RFP response | 03 — Intent | Decision support |
| Slide deck — QBR | 05 — Retention | Existing-customer expansion |

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Propose
Based on doc type + brief topic/intent + (optional) sales context, output in caveman lite:

```
Proposed: Stage 0X — [Stage Name]
Reasoning: [one line — why this stage for this brief]
Goal:      [stage goal]
Confirm (yes / ok / confirm) or override ("stage 03", "stage 01", etc.).
```

### Step 2 — User confirmation
Accept:
- "yes" / "ok" / "confirm" / "approved" → lock the proposed stage
- "stage 0X" / "X" / stage name → override and lock the new stage
- "explain" → output the full stage description and re-ask

### Step 3 — Lock and emit Journey Card

```
JOURNEY CARD

Stage:          0X — [Name]
Goal:           [stage goal]
Reasoning:      [why this stage for this brief]
Content angle:  [angle pulled from stage topic buckets, matched to brief topic]
CTA pattern:    [primary CTA from the table below, matched to stage + doc type]
Risk-reducer:   [supporting line — see table]
Channels:       [hint channels for distribution]
```

The Journey Card is passed to the drafter skill in the chain. The drafter MUST honour the CTA pattern, content angle, and risk-reducer line where the deliverable supports it.

## Output Contract

The drafter skill (ebook / brochure / flyer / sales-collateral) MUST:
1. Use the locked content angle as the brief's positioning.
2. Match the primary CTA exactly to the Journey Card.
3. Include the risk-reducer line beneath the CTA where the deliverable supports it (brochures and flyers always; ebooks on back cover; slide decks on CTA slide).
4. Honour the channel hints if multi-channel adaptation is requested.

If the drafter cannot honour the Journey Card (e.g., user-supplied CTA conflicts), it must halt and ask the user to either accept the Journey Card or re-run `/journey-mapper` with new input.
