## PART 1: BLOG POSTS

### 1.1 Blog Post Structure

*Standard structure all blog posts must follow.*

**1. Title (H1) — primary keyword**
Format: `[Primary keyword phrase] — [secondary angle or audience]`
Example: `Voice Bot vs IVR for Fast-Growing Businesses`
Example: `How NBFCs Are Automating DPD 1-30 Collections in 2026`

**2. Introduction (150–200 words)**
- Hook: one sharp sentence that names the pain or the stakes
- Problem statement: the specific operational reality the reader is living in
- What they'll learn: one sentence on what the post delivers
- Do not tease without delivering. Do not start with "In today's fast-paced world."

*Template:*
```
[Sharp hook — name the pain or the paradox].

[Problem statement — 2–3 sentences describing the ops reality. Use numbers where possible. ₹, %, call volumes, team sizes.]

In this [guide / breakdown / comparison], you'll [learn / see / understand] [specific outcome the reader gets from reading].
```

*Example:*
```
Most e-commerce operations managers running COD confirmation calls know the number: ₹150 per call.
What they don't always know is that 30–40% of those calls result in a returned order anyway.

At 50,000 calls a month, that's ₹75 lakh in call costs — and a returns problem that manual calling
can't fix.

In this guide, you'll see exactly how voice automation is cutting COD-related costs by 60–70%,
and how to deploy it without an engineering team.
```

**3. H2 Sections (3–7 per post)**

*Structure rules:*
- Each H2 should answer one specific question or make one specific point
- Minimum one data point, example, or proof per section
- If comparing two things: use a table
- If listing steps: use numbered list
- If making a claim: follow immediately with evidence

*H2 naming patterns that work for Acefone:*
- "Why [X] Is No Longer Enough" (problem deepening)
- "How [X] Works — and What It Actually Does" (education)
- "The [X] vs [Y] Comparison: What Ops Managers Actually Need to Know" (comparison)
- "What to Look for in a [Category] Platform" (evaluation frame)
- "How [Company type] Are Using [Feature] to [Outcome]" (proof/use case)
- "Common Objections — and What the Data Says" (objection handling)

**4. Conclusion + CTA (100–150 words)**

*Template:*
```
[1–2 sentence summary of the core takeaway — what changed in their thinking after reading this.]

[Bridge to action — 1 sentence connecting the takeaway to AceX specifically, without being abrupt.]

[CTA — one of the standard CTAs from Part 5. Match urgency to the post's trigger context.]
```

### 1.2 Word Count Guidelines

| Content Type | Min | Max | Sweet Spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blog post (educational) | 1,200 | 3,000 | 1,800–2,200 | Longer for high-intent SEO topics (compliance, comparisons) |
| Blog post (thought leadership) | 700 | 1,500 | 900–1,200 | LinkedIn-native length. Punchy, perspective-forward. |
| Blog post (product/use case) | 800 | 1,800 | 1,200–1,500 | Use case-led. Show, don't just tell. |
| Landing page (hero + body) | 400 | 900 | 500–700 | Less is more. Proof blocks do the heavy lifting. |
| Product page | 500 | 1,200 | 700–900 | Feature → benefit → proof per section. |
| Case study | 600 | 1,200 | 800–1,000 | Before / After / How / Result. Always include numbers. |
| Email (nurture) | 120 | 250 | 150–180 | One idea. One CTA. No padding. |
| Email (onboarding) | 80 | 200 | 100–150 | Tell them one thing to do. Nothing else. |
| LinkedIn post (organic) | 150 | 700 | 250–400 | Hook in line 1. Payoff before the fold. |
| LinkedIn ad copy | 50 | 150 | 80–120 | Benefit-first. One claim. One CTA. |
| Google Search ad (headline) | — | 30 chars | — | 3 headlines per ad. |
| Google Search ad (description) | — | 90 chars | — | 2 descriptions per ad. |
| Sales one-pager | 300 | 500 | 350–450 | One ICP, one use case, one outcome, one CTA. |
| Battlecard | 400 | 700 | 500–600 | Must fit one screen. No scrolling in a sales call. |

## PART 8: CONTENT DO'S AND DON'TS

### Do's

**Writing**
- Lead every piece with the pain or the outcome — never with the company or product
- Use specific numbers: ₹150/call, 60–70% reduction, 48 hours, 500–600ms. Vague claims get ignored.
- Write in second person ("your team", "you") in customer-facing content. First person in thought leadership.
