## Headline Formulas

### Outcome-Focused
- `{Achieve outcome} without {pain point}` → *Understand how users experience your site without drowning in numbers*
- `{Achieve outcome} by {how product makes it possible}` → *Generate more leads by seeing which companies visit your site*
- `Turn {input} into {outcome}` → *Turn your hard-earned sales into repeat customers*
- `[Achieve outcome] in [timeframe]` → *Get your tax refund in 10 days*

### Problem-Focused
- `Never {unpleasant event} again` → *Never miss a sales opportunity again*
- `{Question highlighting pain point}` → *Hate returning stuff to Amazon?*
- `Stop [pain]. Start [pleasure].` → *Stop chasing invoices. Start getting paid on time.*

### Audience-Focused
- `{Feature} for {audience}` → *Advanced analytics for Shopify e-commerce*
- `{Feature} for {audience} to {use}` → *An online whiteboard for teams to ideate together*
- `You don't have to {skill} to {outcome}` → *You don't have to be an SEO pro to rank higher*

### Differentiation-Focused
- `The {opposite of usual} way to {outcome}` → *The easiest way to turn your passion into income*
- `The [category] that [differentiator]` → *The CRM that updates itself*

### Proof-Focused
- `[Number] [people] use [product] to [outcome]` → *50,000 marketers use Drip to send better emails*
- `{Key benefit}` → *Sound clear in online meetings*

### Additional Formulas
- `The simple way to {outcome}`
- `Finally, {category} that {benefit}`
- `{Outcome} without {common pain}`
- `Get {benefit} from your {thing}`
- `{Action verb} your {thing} like {admirable example}`
- `What if you could {desirable outcome}?`
- `Everything you need to {outcome}`
- `The {adjective} {category} built for {audience}`

## Landing Page Section Types

### Core Sections

**Hero** — Headline + subheadline, primary CTA, supporting visual, optional social proof bar.

**Social Proof Bar** — Customer logos (recognizable > many), key metric, star rating, short testimonial.

**Problem/Pain** — Articulate their problem better than they can. Create recognition. Hint at cost of not solving.

**Solution/Benefits** — Bridge from problem to solution. 3-5 key benefits (not 10). Each: headline + explanation + proof.

**How It Works** — 3-4 numbered steps. Each: action + outcome. Reduces perceived complexity.

**Final CTA** — Recap value prop, repeat CTA, risk reversal (guarantee/free trial).

### Supporting Sections

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Testimonials | Specific results > vague praise. Name, role, company, photo. |
| Case Studies | Problem → Solution → Results with metrics. |
| Use Cases | Helps visitors self-identify. "For marketers who need X." |
| Personas / "Built For" | Answers "Is this for me?" Explicit audience callouts. |
| FAQ | Address objections. Good for SEO. 5-10 questions. |
| Comparison | vs. competitors or vs. status quo. Tables work well. |
| Integrations | "Works with your stack." Logo wall. |
| Founder Story | Why you built this. Emotional connection. |
| Demo / Product Tour | Interactive demos, video, GIF previews. |
| Guarantee / Risk Reversal | Money-back, free trial, "Cancel anytime." Reduces friction. |
| Stats Section | "10,000+ customers", "4.9/5 rating", "$2M saved." |

## Page Structure Templates

### Feature-Heavy (Weak — avoid)
Hero → Feature 1 → Feature 2 → Feature 3 → CTA
*This is a list, not a persuasive narrative.*

### Varied, Engaging (Strong)
1. Hero with clear value prop
2. Social proof bar
3. Problem/pain section
4. How it works (3 steps)
5. Key benefits (2-3)
6. Testimonial
7. Use cases or personas
8. Comparison to alternatives
9. Case study snippet
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA with guarantee

### Compact Landing Page (ad traffic)
1. Hero (headline, subhead, CTA, image)
2. Social proof bar
3. 3 key benefits with icons
4. Testimonial
5. How it works (3 steps)
6. Final CTA with guarantee

### Enterprise / B2B
1. Hero (outcome-focused headline)
2. Logo bar (recognizable companies)
3. Problem section (business pain)
4. Solution overview
5. Use cases by role/department
6. Security/compliance section
7. Integration logos
8. Case study with metrics
9. ROI/value section
10. Contact/demo CTA

### Product Launch Page
1. Hero with launch announcement
2. Video demo or walkthrough
3. Feature highlights (3-5)
4. Before/after comparison
5. Early testimonials
6. Launch pricing or early access offer
7. CTA with urgency

## Section Writing Tips

### Problem Section
Lead with recognition phrases:
- "You know the feeling..."
- "If you're like most [role]..."
- "Every day, [audience] struggles with..."

Then: specific frustration → time/money wasted → impact.

### Benefits Section
Each benefit needs three parts:
- **Headline**: The outcome they get
- **Body**: How it works (1-2 sentences)
- **Proof**: Number, testimonial, or example (optional)

### How It Works Section
Each step: numbered + simple verb + outcome.
> 1. Connect your tools (2 minutes) → 2. Set preferences → 3. Get automated reports every Monday

### Testimonial Selection
**Use:** Specific results ("increased conversions by 32%"), before/after context, role + company.
**Avoid:** "Great product!", "Love it!", "Easy to use!" — vague = unconvincing.
